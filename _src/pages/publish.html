The Process

Phase 1

Edit for impact

Once the draft is on the page, we refine it. This is where we tighten the narrative, sharpen the ideas, and make sure your voice sounds like you on your best day. We move through a clear editorial process, with room for your feedback at every stage, until we have a clean, publish ready manuscript.

Phase 2

Design the book, inside and out

Next, the manuscript becomes a book. We design a professional cover, typeset the interior, and prepare files for print and digital platforms. You will see proofs at each stage and have a chance to weigh in on look and feel.

Phase 3

Print and distribute

When you approve the design, we set your book up for printing. This includes print on demand and a local print run for launch events. We handle the technical details (things like ISBNs, spine width, and printer specs) so you can focus on the thrilling part: opening that first box of books.

We also pitch your book to organizations where your readers work, so they can buy copies in bulk for their teams and events.

Entrechat Press titles are printed locally for events and hand selling, listed on major online retailers through print on demand, and pitched to independent bookstores and specialty shops where your readers already spend time. Together we will decide what makes sense for your book, your audience, and your goals.

You Own Your Book

Copyright & Ownership

You own your book. Full stop.

When you publish with Entrechat Press, you retain 100% of your copyright and intellectual property. The story, ideas, and content are yours and always will be. Entrechat Press simply receives the right to publish the book in print and digital formats under our imprint.

You can use your book however you choose in your business, speaking, courses, workshops, or future creative projects. Nothing is locked down.

Publishing & ISBNs

All books published through Entrechat Press use ISBNs registered to the imprint. This ensures your book enters the market professionally, with accurate metadata, industry-standard cataloguing, and consistent branding across all editions.

Authors remain the full copyright holders. Entrechat Press simply holds publishing rights for print and digital formats.

Royalties

Authors receive the majority of net royalties from book sales.

Royalty splits are clearly outlined in your publishing agreement and vary depending on production costs, distribution channels, and services included. There are no surprise deductions or vague accounting.

You receive transparent sales reporting and regular royalty payments. Your success is our success.

Rights & Licensing

Authors who work with Entrechat Press keep control over all major rights, including:

  • Digital and print rights
  • Audiobook rights
  • Translation rights
  • Film/TV rights
  • Course or workshop adaptations

If future opportunities come up (foreign rights, audio producers, brand partnerships), we support you without owning or restricting your creative options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this self-publishing?

No.

Entrechat Press operates as a hybrid independent publisher. That means we handle professional editing, design, production, distribution strategy, and publishing under our imprint.

You retain ownership and control, while we bring structure, expertise, and accountability to the process.

It is far more supported than DIY self-publishing, without the loss of control common in traditional publishing.

Is this a vanity press?

Vanity presses are paid to print books.

Entrechat Press is paid to build them properly. We work selectively, invest deeply in each project, and are aligned with authors for the long term.

Our agreements are transparent, our royalties are clearly defined, and our reputation depends on the quality of the books we publish.

Who controls creative decisions?

You do.

We provide guidance, editorial direction, and strategic recommendations, but final creative decisions remain with the author.

This is your book. Our role is to help you make it as strong, clear, and impactful as possible.

Who controls distribution?

Entrechat Press manages distribution for print and digital formats during the term of our agreement.

We handle ISBN registration, metadata, retailer access, and production coordination so your book enters the market professionally. You are kept informed and involved throughout the process, and nothing is done without clarity or consent.

Can I take my book elsewhere in the future?

Yes.

Our publishing agreements are structured to support flexibility. If your goals change in the future, including pursuing a different publisher or edition, those conversations are possible. You are not locked into lifetime rights grabs or restrictive contracts.

Do you help with marketing and promotion?

Yes. Marketing is a core part of what we do.

We plan your launch with you, gather early reader reviews, pitch media, submit to awards and sell copies in bulk to organizations. Then we keep your book visible long after launch week.

Your agreement spells out exactly which services are included.

Who is Entrechat Press best for?

Entrechat Press is best for first-time nonfiction authors, founders, executives, and thought leaders who want a high-quality book that reflects their ideas, values, and credibility.

Why the name Entrechat Press?

No entrechat happens alone, either. You bring the story and the voice. We bring the structure and the experience. This is where courage meets craft.
