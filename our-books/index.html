“White Men, I Need You!”

“White Men, I Need You!” is a no-nonsense guide for the leaders whose voices carry the most weight in the room. Drawing on 20+ years advising leaders and organizations as a DEI consultant, Sema Burney makes the case that allyship isn't a side conversation, it's a leadership skill, and she tells white men exactly what active allyship looks like in practice.

“Thank God I Failed”

“Thank God I Failed” is a memoir for anyone who has ever let a quiet voice in their head drown out everything else. In it, Orlando Da Silva walks readers through the story of how he almost lost his life at the height of his career, helping them see that failure is not the end but often the beginning of something truer.
