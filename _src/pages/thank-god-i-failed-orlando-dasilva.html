“Thank God I Failed” by Orlando Da Silva

He was at the top of his career. And so close to ending his life.

In 2008, Orlando Da Silva almost died by suicide.

He was a senior trial lawyer with aspirations to lead the Ontario Bar Association. But he had spent decades believing a voice in his head that kept getting louder.

“Thank God I Failed” is the story of what happens when that voice nearly wins.

About the Author

Orlando Da Silva is the CEO of the Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada and former President of the Ontario Bar Association. He was named one of the Top 25 Most Influential Lawyers by Canadian Lawyer Magazine and is a recipient of the Law Society Medal. He lives in Uxbridge, Ontario, and plays the violin badly but often.
