How we tell your story

Beyond Templates

Some ghostwriters offer templates. We don't. Just as Adina built this business on her own rules, we believe there's no single right way to structure a book.

Every strong business book needs core elements:

  • Storytelling first (we learn through stories, it's why fables and myths endure)
  • Proof and credibility (how did you develop your expertise?)
  • Tactical "how-to" guidance (not just what to change, but exactly how to do it)

But how you present these elements can vary. You might start with story, add proof, then provide examples. Or begin with proof and weave examples throughout.

Our job is to help you find your natural voice and the structure that fits your book.
