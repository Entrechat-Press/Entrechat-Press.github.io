Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do you do?

We help first-time nonfiction authors write, publish, and market their books. That includes shaping your ideas, crafting the manuscript, managing editing and design, and guiding your launch, so your message reaches the right audience.

What kinds of books do you help write?

We specialize in nonfiction, particularly business, memoir, and thought-leadership books. Many of our clients are first-time authors who want to share their expertise or life story in a way that feels both strategic and personal.

What's the difference between ghostwriting and book coaching?

Ghostwriting means we handle the writing for you, based on in-depth interviews and research. Book coaching means we co-create by guiding you through the writing process, and providing structure, accountability, and editorial feedback while you write.

How long does it take to write a book?

Every project is different, but most ghostwriting and book coaching engagements take 6 months from interviews to completed manuscript. Tack on another month for publishing, and another for marketing.

What's included in your publishing support?

Adina Samuels Inc. owns Entrechat Press, an independent publisher designed for nonfiction authors who want creative control with professional support.

We manage every stage from editing, design, formatting, ISBNs, metadata, and distribution through Amazon, Ingram, and select print partners.

Authors keep 100% of their copyright. See exactly what you own.

Do you also help with marketing?

Yes. We offer launch strategy, author sites, newsletter setup, and LinkedIn thought-leadership content so your message and following grow.

I've already written a draft. Can you edit or refine it?

Absolutely. We offer developmental, line, and copy editing for existing manuscripts.
