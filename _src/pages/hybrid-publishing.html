How Hybrid Publishing Works

Phase 1

Edit for impact

We tighten the narrative, sharpen the ideas, and make sure your voice sounds like you on your best day. You’ll have room for feedback at every stage, until the manuscript is clean and ready to publish.

Phase 2

Design the book, inside and out

We design a professional cover, typeset the interior, and prepare files for print and digital. You’ll see proofs at each stage and weigh in on the look and feel.

Phase 3

Print and distribute

We handle ISBNs, printer specs and retailer setup, with print on demand for online stores and a local print run for launch events. Your book carries the Entrechat Press name.

Phase 4

Launch it

We run your pre-launch marketing campaign and build your waitlist, recruit ARC readers and manage early reviews, create your social media kit, and conduct and share your author interview.

Phase 5

Keep it moving

After launch, we pitch your book to distributors, support bulk sales to organizations where your readers work, and check in every month to make sure momentum doesn’t stall.

We publish a curated list of titles each year. When your book carries the Entrechat name, our reputation is on the line alongside yours.

That’s why we’re motivated to help it succeed long after publication day.

Hybrid publishing works best for authors who want someone in their corner for the long haul, who know their book has an audience but need help reaching it, and who want to focus on being the author rather than the marketer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this self-publishing?

No.

With hybrid publishing, your book is published under the Entrechat Press imprint. We handle editing, design, production, distribution and launch marketing, and you keep ownership of your work. It’s far more supported than DIY self-publishing, without the loss of control common in traditional publishing.

Is this a vanity press?

Vanity presses are paid to print books.

Entrechat Press is paid to build them properly. We work selectively, publish a curated list each year, and our reputation depends on the quality of every book we publish.

Who controls creative decisions?

You do.

We provide guidance, editorial direction and strategic recommendations, but final creative decisions stay with you.

What happens after my book launches?

We keep going. We pitch your book to distributors, support bulk sales outreach, and check in every month to make sure momentum doesn’t stall.

How is this different from assisted self-publishing?

With assisted self-publishing, we produce your book and hand you the wheel at launch. With hybrid publishing, we also run your launch campaign and keep working to get your book in front of readers after it’s published. More on assisted self-publishing.

How do I know which option is right for me?

If you’re asking yourself “how do I make sure this book reaches the people it’s meant for?”, that’s the hybrid conversation. Book a discovery call and we’ll help you figure out which option fits where you are.
