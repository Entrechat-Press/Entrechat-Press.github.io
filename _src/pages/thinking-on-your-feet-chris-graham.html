Thinking On Your Feet by Chris Graham

You know the answer, but struggle to find the right words in the moment.

About the Author

Chris Graham teaches lawyers and other professionals how to connect with their clients and teams. Since 2016, he’s trained over 20,000 people at North America’s leading law firms, large and small companies, government agencies, and charities.

He studied at Oxford University, the Henry N. R. Jackman Faculty of Law, University of Toronto, and Acadia University, and is a graduate of the stand-up comedy program at The Second City in Toronto.
