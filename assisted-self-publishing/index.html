How Assisted Self-Publishing Works

Step 1

Design the book

We design a professional cover and format the interior for print and ebook, so your book looks like it belongs on the shelf next to anything from a major publisher.

Step 2

Get it set up

We help you register your ISBNs and set up distribution through Amazon, Ingram and Draft2Digital, so your book is available to online retailers, bookstores and ebook platforms.

Step 3

Launch-ready

We set up your Amazon Author Central profile and A+ content, and project-manage every detail through to launch day.

Step 4

Hand you the wheel

Once your book is live, it’s in your hands. The marketing, the outreach, the reviews and the momentum are yours to drive. We set you up for success, then step back.

If you know what you’re doing on the marketing side and just need a professional team to produce a great book, this is your option.

Assisted self-publishing works well for authors who already have a clear plan for getting their book in front of readers: a speaking calendar, a client base, a community or a platform of their own.

You get the polish of a professionally produced book, and you stay in the driver’s seat from there.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does assisted self-publishing include editing?

No. This option starts from a polished, publish-ready manuscript.

If your manuscript still needs work, we offer editing and book coaching, or you can choose hybrid publishing, which includes editorial support.

Where will my book be sold?

We set up distribution through Amazon, Ingram and Draft2Digital. Together they reach online retailers, bookstores, libraries and the major ebook platforms.

Do you help with marketing?

Not with this option. Once your book is live, the marketing, outreach and reviews are yours to drive.

If you’d like a team running your launch and keeping your book visible afterwards, hybrid publishing is the better fit.

Who manages the process?

We do. You get full project management from finished manuscript through to launch, so you always know what’s happening and what’s next.

How do I know which option is right for me?

If you have a clear plan for reaching readers, assisted self-publishing gives you a professional book and leaves the rest to you. If you’re asking “how do I make sure this book reaches the people it’s meant for?”, that’s the hybrid conversation.

Book a discovery call and we’ll help you figure out which fits where you are.
