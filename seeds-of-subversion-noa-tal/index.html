"Seeds of Subversion" by Noa Tal

Something is wrong. You can feel it. Since October 7, Jew-hatred has spread faster, louder, and more effectively than at any point in modern history. It’s fracturing the left, and it’s fracturing the right.

This didn’t happen by accident.

“Seeds of Subversion” names what’s really going on, covering who is behind it, how they’re doing it, and what can be done about it.

Drawing on leading scholars, researchers, and insiders, Noa Tal deconstructs the antisemitic claims flooding our feeds and traces them back to their source.

About the Author

Noa Tal is a technology lawyer and proud Israeli-American living in the heart of Silicon Valley with her husband and three beautiful children. She is a passionate defender of Jewish values and their fundamental importance to Western Civilization, starting with the most important one: the infinite worth of each human being and his God-given right to live freely and with purpose.
