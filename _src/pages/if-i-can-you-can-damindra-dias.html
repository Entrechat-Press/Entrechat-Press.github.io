"If I Can, You Can" by Damindra Dias

Damindra Dias arrived in Canada with her spouse Shelton, four suitcases, and an unwavering determination to succeed.

Over the following decades, Damindra advanced through the ranks at two globally recognized companies, Kellogg and Coca-Cola, building teams and forging new paths across Canada, India, and the United States.

Damindra's story continues into retirement, where she has spent 23 years as a volunteer Career Coach and Mentor. She guides both new immigrants and local graduates in overcoming their lack of “relevant Canadian work experience”.

Damindra believes in the power of effective networking. She helps job seekers understand the market they are entering, identify the skills they bring to the table, and bridge the gaps needed to achieve their career goals in their chosen profession.

"If I Can, You Can" is both Damindra's story and an invitation for readers to recognize their own potential.

All net proceeds go to three women’s causes: CAMH’s womeninmind (Canadian sales); Aspire For Her (Indian sales); and Women in Management Sri Lanka, funding a girls’ probation centre (Sri Lankan sales).

About the Author

Damindra Dias is a Sri Lankan-born Canadian executive and career coach who spent decades in senior leadership roles at Kellogg and Coca-Cola Company across Canada, India, and the United States. After retiring from corporate life, she joined the Progress Career Planning Institute in Toronto as a volunteer career coach and mentor, a role she has held for over 20 years. She has supported hundreds of new immigrants, job seekers, and recent graduates in building careers they are proud of. If I Can, You Can is her first book.
