Writing a book is not the same as publishing one. And publishing a book is not the same thing as marketing one.

At Entrechat Press, we approach book marketing the same way we approach writing and publishing: thoughtfully, strategically, and with respect for your time and energy.

Our goal is to help the right readers find your work, understand why it matters, and stay connected to your ideas long after launch.

We believe that the most effective book marketing:

  • Starts long before launch
  • Is rooted in ideas, not gimmicks
  • Builds trust before asking for attention
  • Compounds over time rather than spiking and disappearing

Your book is a long-term asset. And everything we offer is designed to support that reality.
